# Sustainable Finance Transition Planning (ISO 32212:2026)

## Applicability

ISO 32212:2026 is designed for financial institutions and relevant financial activities. NOLI Vendaz must therefore make an explicit scope decision rather than claiming universal applicability.

Apply this framework when:

- the operating legal entity is a financial institution;
- NOLI directly provides financing, credit, investment, insurance or capital-market activity;
- NOLI controls or materially influences a financial activity to which management elects to apply ISO 32212;
- a customer, regulator, funder or group policy contractually requires it.

If none apply, mark the standard `CONDITIONAL / NOT CURRENTLY IN CERTIFICATION SCOPE`, retain the rationale and review at least annually or after business-model change.

## Governance

Where applicable, management shall approve:

- transition-planning scope and accountable executive;
- current-position assessment methodology;
- climate-related risks, opportunities, dependencies and impacts considered material;
- transition objectives and measurable targets;
- time horizons and baseline year;
- data sources, quality limitations and estimation methods;
- financing/activity levers within NOLI's control or influence;
- engagement/escalation strategy for counterparties/providers;
- internal and external communication controls;
- performance review and corrective-action process.

## Current-position assessment

Document the operating/business model and relevant financial activities, then assess:

- direct operational emissions where data is available;
- material supplier/cloud/provider impacts where relevant;
- financed or facilitated activity if NOLI becomes directly involved in financing;
- climate-related physical and transition risks affecting service continuity, providers, customers and assets;
- regulatory/market changes affecting products or financial counterparties;
- data gaps and uncertainty.

Do not invent emissions precision. Estimated data must identify method, source, confidence/limitations and owner.

## Objectives and targets

Targets must be measurable, time-bound, linked to the approved scope and supported by an implementation path. Example categories, only where relevant:

- renewable-energy/cloud-region sourcing preference where evidence is available;
- hardware/vendor lifecycle and energy-efficiency requirements;
- lower-energy device/communications choices;
- provider/supplier climate disclosure coverage;
- climate-resilience requirements in supplier/continuity assessments;
- transition criteria for any future lending/financing products;
- data-quality improvement for climate metrics.

Targets are approved by management, not generated by the application.

## Integration into decisions

Where applicable, procurement, provider onboarding, financing/product decisions and major infrastructure changes should consider approved transition objectives alongside quality, security, service, cost and continuity factors.

Climate criteria must not silently override security, customer protection, financial integrity or legal obligations. Trade-offs are documented and approved.

## Counterparty/provider engagement

For material counterparties, define:

- requested data/evidence;
- expected transition/resilience practices;
- review cadence;
- escalation where material gaps persist;
- contractual or sourcing levers where proportionate.

## Communication and claims

Any external net-zero, climate-alignment or transition claim must be supported by approved scope, methodology, baseline, target, performance evidence and limitations. Marketing language may not exceed the evidence.

## Performance review

At least annually where applicable:

1. review current position and material risks/opportunities;
2. measure performance against targets;
3. assess data quality and methodological changes;
4. review effectiveness of transition levers and engagement;
5. record nonconformities/shortfalls;
6. assign corrective actions;
7. update plans and communications;
8. include outcomes in management review and internal audit.

## Application support

The NOLI application may support ISO 32212 evidence by capturing provider/supplier metadata, approved targets, evidence links, review dates and decision rationale. It must not calculate or assert a compliant transition plan without organization-approved methodology and data.
